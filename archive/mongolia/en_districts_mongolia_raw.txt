A '''district''' or '''sum''' ({{IPAc-en|s|ʊ|m}}; {{langx|mn|[[wikt:сум#Mongolian|сум]]}} {{MongolUnicode|ᠰᠤᠮᠤ}}) is a second-level administrative subdivision of [[Mongolia]]. The 21 [[provinces of Mongolia]] are divided into 330 [[Sum (administrative division)|sum]]s.<ref>{{Cite web |title=21 аймгийн 330 сум, нийслэлийн 152 хороо болон хилийн чанад дахь ДТГ-ын бүртгэлийн үйл ажиллагаанд ашиглаж буй тоног төхөөрөмжийг шинэчлэнэ |url=http://burtgel.gov.mn/archive/index.php/2424-21-6 |access-date=2023-09-21 |website=burtgel.gov.mn}}</ref>

On average, each district administers a territory of {{convert|4200|km2|abbr=on}} with about 5,000 inhabitants, primarily nomadic herders. Its{{clarify|date=April 2017}} total revenue is 120 million [[Mongolian tögrög|Tögrög]], 90% of which comes from national subsidies.

Each district is again subdivided into ''bags'' (brigades; sometimes spelled ''baghs''<ref name="mongol">Montsame News Agency. ''Mongolia''. 2006, Foreign Service office of [[Montsame News Agency]], {{ISBN|99929-0-627-8}}, p. 46</ref>). Most bags are of an entirely virtual nature. Their purpose is to sort the families of nomads in the district into groups, without a permanent human settlement.

Officially, and occasionally on maps, many district seats (''sum'' centers) bear a name different from that of the district. However, in practice the district seat (sum center) is most often referred to under the name of the district, to the point of the official name of the district seat (sum center) being unknown even to the locals.
