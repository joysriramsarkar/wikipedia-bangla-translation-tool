This is a '''list of [[Tamil language]] films''' produced in the [[Tamil cinema]] in India that were released in 2014.

Post-amendment to the Tamil Nadu Entertainment Tax Act 1939 on 27 September 2011, gross jumped to 130 per cent of net for films with non-Tamil titles and U certificates as well.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.stationeryprinting.tn.gov.in/extraordinary/2011/368-Ex-IV-2.pdf |title=Entertainment tax doubled to 30% (from 15%) for non-Tamil titles and U certificates |access-date=7 February 2019 |archive-date=9 February 2019 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190209180104/http://www.stationeryprinting.tn.gov.in/extraordinary/2011/368-Ex-IV-2.pdf |url-status=live }}</ref> Commercial Taxes Department disclosed {{INR}}82.97 crore in entertainment tax revenue for the year.<ref>[{{Cite web |url=https://ctd.tn.gov.in/documents/10184/20741/ADMINISTRATIVE+REPORT+2017-2018/748773c3-2f25-4192-a416-268eb4931cd9?version=1.1 |title=CTD reports {{INR}}82.97 crore ET collection in 2014–15 |access-date=19 January 2019 |archive-date=19 January 2019 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190119121141/https://ctd.tn.gov.in/documents/10184/20741/ADMINISTRATIVE+REPORT+2017-2018/748773c3-2f25-4192-a416-268eb4931cd9?version=1.1 |url-status=live }} CTD reports {{INR}}82.97 crore ET collection in 2014–15]</ref>
